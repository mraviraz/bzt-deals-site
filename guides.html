Original deal guides

Practical notes for smarter Amazon India shopping. Updated 27 September 2026.

Why crossed-out MRP can mislead

MRP is not the same as a product's normal selling price. Compare recent prices before calling something a deal.

What makes a real price drop

A real drop is supported by recent typical prices, not just an inflated reference price.

Coupons versus price drops

Coupons may be account-, location-, or quantity-dependent, so treat them as conditional savings.

How to read an alert

Check current price, seller, delivery, variant, coupon conditions, and stock before buying.

New, used, and renewed

Condition matters. Confirm the listing condition on Amazon before checkout.

Why prices change quickly

Inventory, sellers, lightning deals, and promotions can change a price quickly.

Bank offers and eligibility

Card discounts may require a particular card, EMI plan, or minimum cart value.

Avoiding the wrong variant

Size, colour, storage, pack count, and seller can change the price.

Why we suppress repeats

A cooldown keeps one product from dominating the channel.

Checkout checklist

Verify seller, condition, delivery, final price, coupon, card terms, and returns.
